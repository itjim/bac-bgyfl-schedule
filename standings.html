Bloomingdale Bears BGYFL Standings

BAC team overview and full division standings

Standings Notice: This live view is provided as a convenience using BGYFL data. Records may change as scores are reported or corrected. Confirm official records, playoff eligibility, seeding, and tiebreakers with your team coach, BAC, or BGYFL before relying on this information.

Position is not playoff seeding: Positions follow the BGYFL website’s table order: win percentage, wins, then team name. Ties count as half a win. Teams with equal records may be separated alphabetically; this does not establish an official league tiebreaker.

8U coach / board review: BGYFL does not display 8U standings on its public website. This view includes 8U API records for coach and board review. This page is not access restricted.

How to use: Start with the BAC overview, then select a team’s “View division standings” button to compare it with the full division. BAC teams are highlighted in blue. Select a season for historical data, and use Refresh to retrieve current records. Source: BGYFL live API; retrieval time appears below.
